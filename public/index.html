            01 / DELIVERY EXPERIENCE
            
CoreRunner

            Live interactive demo
          

          

            
One delivery. Several connected experiences.

            

              A fictional Atlanta courier service brings customer booking,
              dispatch, courier handoffs, and recipient tracking into one
              application. The challenge is keeping each person informed while
              making the next action clear.
            

            

              

                
Built so far

                

                  Persistent bookings and quotes, courier assignments, PIN and
                  signature handoffs, delivery photos, grocery readiness review,
                  and simulated tracking.
                

              

              

                
Current focus

                

                  The hosted portfolio demo connects customer booking, dispatch,
                  courier workflows, and tracking. Email, payments, and route
                  positions are simulated; use sample information only.
                

              

            

            

              ReactExpressSQLite
